# CIS-344-Organic-Juice-Bar
I created a database for a Organic juice Bar with mySQL and drew notation diagrams.


I chose to take inspiration from a juice bar business here in New York named Juice Generation and created a mini world of what the business would look like on mySQL and ERDs. For them, I picked the most important data that makes up the business like data on stores, customers, orders, and items.
Some challenges that I overcame was understanding what data should be foreign keys or not. I eventually overcame this by searching up some examples and studying the ER diagrams that I created before finalizing my database on mySQL.
